# Spotted. privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

**Short version: Spotted. collects nothing about you.**

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.**
- **No personal data leaves your browser.** Spotted. never reads your browsing history, your tabs or the pages you visit.
- **What it downloads:** once a day it fetches a public list of ad picks, quotes and awards from `georgepohl2-design.github.io`. That request carries no information about you beyond what any web request does (your IP address reaches GitHub's servers, which is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).
- **What it stores, only on your computer** (Chrome's extension storage): the date you last opened it (for the blue "1" that marks a new pick), the date of the last midday alert, whether the test alert on the setup page worked, the last quote shown (so you don't get the same one twice) and a cached copy of the picks for when you're offline. Remove the extension and it's all gone.
- **Notifications** are made by your own Chrome on your own computer. Nothing is sent to a server to do that.
- **Email (optional, coming soon):** if you sign up for the weekly email, your address is held by the email service you sign up through, under its own privacy policy. The extension itself never sees it.
- **Feedback (optional):** the "Got feedback?" link, and the page that opens if you uninstall, go to a Google Form. It doesn't ask for your email or a sign-in. What you type there is held by Google Forms under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The extension itself sends nothing.
- **Links** in the popup open the publisher's or brand's own site, which has its own privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/georgepohl2-design/spotted-daily-ads/issues
