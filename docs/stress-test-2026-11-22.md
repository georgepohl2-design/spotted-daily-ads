## What the test showed

- **Dropped one:** BARK naming rescue dogs "Air Fryer" and "Robot Vacuum" for Black Friday. A great idea, but no source names an agency, so the rule says skip it.
- **Dropped Target too:** on a second look, Marketing Dive published it on 21 Oct, so it's over four weeks old. The checker would have blocked it anyway.
- **Some credits are messy.** LEGO, Johnnie Walker and KitKat all have several agencies credited. We use the lead agency the source names.
- **Adweek and Campaign are mostly paywalled.** They're good for finding ideas, but not for "Read more". Creative Salon, Ads of the World, Marketing Dive, DesignRush, LBB and ContentGrip all open free.
